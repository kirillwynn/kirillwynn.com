# NNNN: Title

ADR filenames use `NNNN-kebab-case.md`.

- **Status:** Proposed / Accepted / Rejected / Superseded
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Owners:** Names or roles

## Context

What problem or constraint requires a decision?

## Decision drivers

What criteria determine a good outcome?

## Considered options

Which credible alternatives were evaluated?

## Decision

What was decided, and why?

## Consequences

What becomes easier, harder, required, or prohibited?

## Validation

How will the decision be verified?

## Risks

What can fail, and how is risk controlled?

## Follow-ups

What later work or decisions are required?

## Supersedes / Superseded by

Link related ADRs, or state `None`.
